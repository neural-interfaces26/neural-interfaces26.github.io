Additional opportunities

Internships.

San Francisco

Alljoined may offer the Track 1 winner a paid summer internship in San Francisco.

  • Eligibility bar: top-3 accuracy above 50% in the best quartile of subjects.
  • Separate from ranking: this threshold does not change the competition ranking.
  • Offer: meeting the threshold guarantees an internship discussion. Lower scores may still be considered.

Track 1 has no cash prize.

Toronto

The Sleep onset winner may choose the $2,000 first prize or a Toronto internship. Details will be announced.

Prizes depend on eligibility and the reproducibility audit. Read the rules and ethics policy. Winners present at the Brain and Body Workshop at NeurIPS in Sydney.
