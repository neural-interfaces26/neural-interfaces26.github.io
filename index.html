One benchmark. Four real shifts.

Biosignal foundation models claim to transfer across subjects, sessions, and devices with limited per-task training. This challenge tests that claim against strong task-specific baselines.

The four-track design separates two claims usually conflated in the literature: single-task performance and shift robustness. One harness and one reproducibility audit hold evaluation fixed, so a per-track score can be attributed to the model rather than the protocol.

01

Cross-stimulus

EEG-to-IMG

Decodes which image a subject is viewing from a single EEG epoch, evaluated as ranking against held-out candidates. Targets are frozen DINOv2-giant embeddings, so the difficulty is in the EEG side, not in re-learning the image space. The controlled shift is cross-stimulus: test stimuli are unseen during training.

Visual retrieval, DINOv2, RSVP, object viewing

Metric: Top-5 acc. Sponsor: AlljoinedView track
02

Cross-session

BCI decoding

Decodes a cued mental command (motor imagery, mental calculation, word association) from short EEG windows. The controlled shift is cross-session: models train on early sessions and are scored on later sessions of the same subject, with no per-session recalibration allowed.

Motor imagery, mental math, word association, session drift

Metric: Balanced acc. Sponsor: Meta FAIR Brain & AIView track
03

Cross-device

Sleep onset

Predicts the latency in seconds from recording start to the first stable N2 epoch, on consumer-grade wearable EEG. The controlled shift is cross-device: home-wearable channels and montages instead of the clinical polysomnography setups of the seed corpora. Precise timing replaces full staging because the wearable signal is too sparse to support per-epoch hypnogram reconstruction.

Sleep onset, wearable EEG, Muse, home monitoring

Metric: W-bMAE (s). Sponsor: InteraXonView track
04

Cross-user

EMG-to-Text

Decodes typed keystrokes from wristband surface EMG as a sequence-transduction problem. The controlled shift is cross-user: 100+ held-out users vary in forearm anatomy, typing strategy, and sensor re-placement, so a model that overfits one user's geometry fails the test.

EMG typing, sequence transduction, wristbands, QWERTY

Metric: CER (%). Sponsor: Meta Reality LabsView track

14 seed datasets + 4 new releases for 2026 (Graz/BrainHero and Muse Sleep-Onset for training; Alljoined eval and emg2qwerty eval as hidden test cohorts that mirror their public parents). The 4 new cohorts become downloadable via NeuralBench when the competition opens Sep 16, 2026; the seed datasets are available now as starter-kit or analog data for pipeline development. All remain under their original providers' licenses. Sleep annotations are expert-scored; stimulus and keystroke labels are machine-derived. Leakage is held out by subject, session, stimulus, and keystroke. Ground truth stays confidential, and organizers re-run the top three. NeuralBench-EEG-Core v1.0. From release to sealed replay.

The competition runs Sep 16 - Nov 16, 2026 (AoE). Starter kit, baselines, datasets, and the scoring container are released at the start; public validation and sealed evaluation run across one window.

  1. Sep 16, 2026

    Submissions open

    Five warm-up submissions per day. Fixed Codabench H100/H200 container with a 60-minute inference budget.

  2. Nov 16, 2026 (AoE)

    Submissions close

    Two submissions per day in the sealed final phase. Ground truth stays confidential.

  3. After the summer

    Audit and final ranking

    Top-3 are re-run on the sealed split with ±2σ tolerance; final-ranking dates are announced later in 2026.

  4. Dec 11 or 12, 2026

    Prizes in Sydney

    Winners present at Foundation Models for the Brain and Body, the NeurIPS workshop in Sydney.
