Built on open source

The open-source stack.

Every layer of this challenge is open. NeuralBench defines the tasks, datasets, and reference baselines; Benchopt is the evaluation framework and starting kits; Codabench hosts submissions and leaderboards. Underneath, braindecode, MOABB, EEGDash, and NEMAR supply the models and the public data. Nothing here is a black box — you can read, run, and extend all of it.
