San Francisco · Alljoined

Alljoined is prepared to offer the first-place winner a paid summer internship in San Francisco. Specific details are to be determined with the candidate.

  • Eligibility bar: top-3 accuracy above 50% in the best quartile of subjects.
  • Separate from ranking: this bar is separate from Track 1’s top-5 competition ranking.
  • Consideration: a score below the bar does not rule out consideration; above the bar guarantees that Alljoined will negotiate the internship with the candidate.

Track 1 has no cash prize.

Toronto · Muse

The Sleep onset winner chooses $2,000 or an internship in Toronto for their representative. Second and third place each receive $2,000. Internship details will be announced.

Cash allocation: $18,000 when the Track 3 winner chooses $2,000 cash; $16,000 when they choose the Toronto internship. Internships are outside the cash total.

Prizes depend on eligibility and the reproducibility audit. Read the rules and ethics policy. Winners present at the Brain and Body Workshop at NeurIPS in Sydney.
