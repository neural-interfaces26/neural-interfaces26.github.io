Awards & prizes.

Each task-specific track awards a USD $2,500 cash prize to the top three teams and travel support for one representative per top-3 team to present at the NeurIPS workshop in Sydney. Some tracks may add a 6-month internship offer at the sponsor company (TBC).

Per-track prizes

$2,500 each to the top three teams per track.

Each task-specific track has its own sponsor and the same cash prize structure: USD $2,500 to first, second, and third place, plus travel support for one representative per top-3 team to present at the NeurIPS workshop in Sydney.

Track 05 FOUNDATION

Foundation-transfer grand prize.

The cross-modality winner. A single shared biosignal encoder evaluated on the EEG-to-IMG, BCI, Sleep, and EMG tracks at once. Rewards the team whose model is the most generally useful across both EEG and surface EMG, not the most specialized on any one. Hosted by Yneuro. Foundation-prize cash is finalised at NeurIPS opening; the per-track 1st-place baseline ($2,500) applies if no additional sponsor is named.

Ethics

This competition follows the NeurIPS Code of Ethics.

Consent, mental privacy, and dataset-controller chains are operational baselines, not afterthoughts. See the ethics page for the full statement, the empirical limits on non-invasive decoding, and the per-track consent posture.
