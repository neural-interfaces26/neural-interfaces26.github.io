## Biography verification

- Warren's page describes machine-learning and neuroscience research at Meta, a neuroscience PhD at Columbia, and internships at DeepMind and Facebook Reality Labs. This is the researcher associated with emg2pose, not the pastor of the same name. His [professional profile](https://www.linkedin.com/in/richard-warren-966036170) also links his personal website and discusses the emg2pose work.
- Tesileanu's page describes research software engineering in Meta's CTRL team, a background in theoretical physics, and mathematical modeling of neural circuits and learning rules. His [former lab's people page](https://neural-circuits-and-algorithms.github.io/people/) also lists his move to Meta. The older [Institute for Advanced Study profile](https://www.ias.edu/scholars/tiberiu-tesileanu) describes his 2011-2014 membership and is not used as a current job biography.
- Other organizers' existing biographies and affiliation text were preserved. The four track-lead labels and Arnault Caillet's additional EMG role are supported by the proposal inventory.

## Portrait provenance and recency

These are authentic portraits currently displayed on the researchers' own websites. Neither page provides a reliable photo capture date; neither image is claimed to be a 2026 photo or definitively the newest photograph available. Search crawl dates and website copyright years are not photo dates. The personal-site portraits were selected over unverified image-search results and historical institutional portraits.

The JPEGs are downloaded source files without visual alteration; the directory uses CSS to fit them into consistent portrait frames. Source links are visible on both cards. No generative portrait or substitute identity was used.
